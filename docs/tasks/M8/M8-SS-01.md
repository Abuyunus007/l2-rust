## Goal
`SevenSigns`: the cycle (competition, results, seal validation), registration, seal stones and contributions, the seals and their effects (avarice, gnosis, strife: spawns, shop prices, siege door and ticket effects, the M7 'neutral state' of the castle NPCs), Ancient Adena, the dawn/dusk priests and the catacomb/necropolis gatekeepers (built in like CLAN-02's `ClanMaster`), `SSQStatus`, the `FOLLOW-UP(Seven Signs)` markers. **Side by side (D-53):** the global rows of Seven Signs (the cycle changes, `seven_signs_status`, and the whole-table `seven_signs` saves) are written only with `CycleDatabaseWrites = True` (`config/SideBySide.ini`, Rust-only, default False, loader in game-server); with it off Kain keeps them in memory, re-reads them at start and when they are shown (D-50), and writes per-player rows only where Java writes them per player.
